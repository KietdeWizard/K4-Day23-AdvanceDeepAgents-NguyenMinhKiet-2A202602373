# Survey about LLM Agents and Tool Use

## TL;DR
Large Language Models (LLMs) are being increasingly employed as tool-using agents across various industries. Despite their effectiveness in enhancing automation and personalization, challenges persist, particularly in tool integration and long-horizon task execution. Continued research is vital for improving their adaptability and minimizing the risks associated with their deployment.

## Background
The rapid evolution of LLMs has seen their applications expand across diverse sectors such as healthcare, automotive, and political analysis. These models are leveraged for their ability to process large datasets and generate insights that are integral for decision-making processes [1][2]. However, as these applications grow, so too do the complexities associated with integrating tool use capabilities [3].

## Current Applications of LLM Agents
LLM agents have significantly impacted industries like healthcare, where they assist in diagnostics and personalized treatment plans, and automotive, where they facilitate predictive maintenance and virtual assistance [2][1]. Their adaptability in executing various tasks has also been demonstrated in educational and e-commerce sectors [1].

### Industrial Applications
In the automotive industry, LLMs aid in real-time navigation and event-based data processing, while in manufacturing, they automate order processing [2]. Such applications showcase the versatility of LLM agents in reducing manual workloads and enhancing user experiences [2].

### Political Analysis
LLMs are also applied in analyzing political lobbying, showcasing their potential in understanding complex political strategies. This application highlights the progression of LLM usage beyond traditional industry confines [1].

## Integration of Tool Use in LLMs
Research indicates that integrating tool use within LLMs enhances their long-term performance capacities. Nevertheless, there exists a gap in comprehensive multi-turn experimentation, highlighting a critical area for further exploration [3].

### Tool Use Performance
Tools like ToolSandbox and HyperAgent are designed to evaluate and enhance tool-use capabilities, addressing issues such as redundancy and tool-selection efficiency [4]. These frameworks are pivotal in fine-tuning LLMs for complex task execution.

## Challenges in Tool Use
Despite advancements, LLMs face challenges like performance degradation with context expansion and the so-called "tool-use tax," which highlights inefficiencies in tool integration [5]. Potential solutions include tool merging and context-aware filtering to improve tool necessity cognition [4].

## Trends and Open Problems
While LLMs continue to revolutionize automation across various sectors, there remains a pressing need for more sophisticated tool-use strategies and ethical considerations in their deployment. Addressing scalability and security concerns will be crucial for the sustainable growth of LLM technologies in the future.
